Frequently Asked Questions

What is Natural Sanitation? +
Natural Sanitation is a professional trash can cleaning service. We use a custom-built trailer with 300° hot water and 4,500 PSI pressure washing to deep-clean and sanitize your bins. We come to you, do all the work, and haul away all the dirty water.
How often will you clean my cans? +
It depends on your plan. The Quarterly Plan includes 3 cleanings per year — Spring, Summer, and Fall. The Monthly Plan includes 9 cleanings per year, every month from March through November.
What if I buy an annual plan and the season is already over? +
No problem at all — your plan simply carries over and kicks off at the start of next spring (March). You won't lose a single cleaning. We've been doing it this way for over 6 years and have handled this for more than 5,000 customers. Sign up any time and we'll take care of the rest.
What do I need to do on cleaning day? +
Just put your cans at the end of your driveway before we arrive. We'll text you the day before as a reminder. You don't need to be home — we handle everything and leave your cans fresh and clean.
Is the cleaning safe for my family and pets? +
Yes! We use hot water and eco-friendly cleaning agents. Our rodent repellent is also eco-friendly and safe for children and pets. No harsh chemicals are used in or around your property.
What if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning? +
We stand behind every cleaning with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, we'll come back and re-clean your cans at no charge — or give you a full refund. No questions asked.
How does billing work? +
You pay once per year via Stripe. Your plan renews annually. You can cancel anytime with no fees and no contracts. Simple and straightforward.
What areas do you service? +
We service the Tri-County area of Southeast Michigan — Oakland, Macomb, and Wayne counties. If you're unsure whether we service your area, contact us and we'll let you know.
What is the return policy? +
We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with your cleaning, contact us within 7 days and we'll re-clean for free or issue a full refund.
