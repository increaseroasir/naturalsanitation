Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Natural Sanitation? +
Natural Sanitation is a professional trash can cleaning service. We use a custom-built trailer with 300° hot water and 4,500 PSI pressure washing to deep-clean and sanitize your bins. We come to you, do all the work, and haul away all the dirty water.
How often will you clean my cans? +
The Quarterly Plan includes 3 cleanings per year — Spring, Summer, and Fall. The Monthly Plan includes 9 cleanings per year, every month from March through November.
What if I buy a plan and the season is already over? +
No problem — your plan carries over and kicks off at the start of next spring (March). You won’t lose a single cleaning. Sign up any time and we’ll take care of the rest.
What do I need to do on cleaning day? +
Just put your cans at the end of your driveway before we arrive. We’ll text you the day before as a reminder. You don’t need to be home — we handle everything.
Is the cleaning safe for my family and pets? +
Yes! We use hot water and eco-friendly cleaning agents. Our rodent repellent is also eco-friendly and safe for children and pets. No harsh chemicals.
What if I’m not satisfied? +
100% satisfaction guarantee. If you’re not happy, we’ll come back and re-clean for free — or give you a full refund. No questions asked.
What areas do you service? +
We service the Tri-County area of Southeast Michigan — Oakland, Macomb, and Wayne counties.
